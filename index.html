40Hz flicker test

Version 3

Warning: flashing light. Not for anyone with a history of photosensitive epilepsy or seizures.

In the headset: dim grey first while the frame rate is set and checked (usually 1 to 3 seconds, up to 10 the first time). Then it flickers black/white at 40Hz. If it turns solid red, the headset is not running at 80Hz and no flicker is shown. Press the Meta button to exit early.
