40Hz picture frame room

Room version 1

Warning: flashing light. Not for anyone with a history of photosensitive epilepsy or seizures.

A simple room. Only the picture on the far wall flickers. In the headset the picture is grey while the frame rate is set and checked (usually 1 to 3 seconds, up to 10 the first time), then flickers black/white at 40Hz. If the picture turns solid red, the headset did not reach 80Hz and no flicker is shown. Press the Meta button to exit early.
